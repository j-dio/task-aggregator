# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University and college students in the Philippines juggling academic assignments from multiple platforms simultaneously. Context: switching between Moodle (UVEC) and Google Classroom throughout the day, often on a phone, under deadline pressure.

## Product Purpose

TapO(1) aggregates tasks from UVEC (Moodle iCal) and Google Classroom into a single Kanban action board — one place for what's due, what's in progress, and what's done. Eliminates the cognitive overhead of checking multiple platforms and lets students focus on the work itself.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, calm, reliable. Confidence through clarity. Like a well-organized planner that never panics.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS / enterprise dashboards (Salesforce, Jira, Monday.com) — too formal, blue-dominant, feature-heavy
- Generic productivity white-label (Asana, ClickUp clones) — interchangeable, no soul
- Bubbly student apps — pastel overload, sticker aesthetic, condescending
- Heavy dark-mode dev tools — Linear / Vercel terminal energy, too cold for academic context

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — every visual element earns its place by helping the student understand their workload
2. **Calm confidence** — the interface stays composed under stress; no alarm-bell aesthetics even for overdue tasks
3. **Structural honesty** — hierarchy through space and weight before color; size and proximity do the communicating
4. **Friction-free flow** — interactions feel immediate and predictable; primary actions are always reachable
5. **Mobile-first realism** — students are on phones; the desktop view is the enhanced version, not the default

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Adequate contrast on all text including muted states. Reduced motion respected (prefers-reduced-motion already implemented). Touch targets minimum 44×44px.
